Project 01

Morning
Productivity

A short-form video script focusing on mental clarity and the power of starting with a single goal.

View Full Script PDF

Direct Download

[Hook]

Do you wake up feeling like you have so much to do... but no idea where to start?

[Body]

Most of us begin our mornings by reaching for our phones. Notifications, messages, social media—it all floods in before we've even had a moment to think for ourselves.

Instead, ask yourself: What is the one thing I need to get done today?

[CTA]

Set one goal. And see how different your day feels.
