UI/UX Case Study

Cineflix
App Design

Duration: 4 Weeks

Tool: Figma

Overview

Cineflix is a movie discovery and watchlist app designed to help users track, explore, and organize films.

Problem

Users struggle to keep track of movies across platforms and browse films efficiently by genre.

Target Users

  • • Movie Enthusiasts
  • • Content Organizers
  • • Casual Viewers

Interactive Prototype

Interact with the Figma Prototype above

The Solution

Cineflix offers a clean, dark-themed structured interface focusing on poster-rich visuals to minimize eye strain and maximize the cinematic feel.

Dark Theme UI

Reduces eye strain for late-night movie browsing sessions.

Card System

A structured design system for easy content digestion.

Key Features

01

Splash & Onboarding

Smooth entry with minimal authentication to get users to the content faster.

02

Watchlist Management

A centralized place to save and organize movies for later viewing.

03

Genre Discovery

Curated sections for Sci-Fi, Horror, and Trending releases.

Challenges

"Balancing high content density with readability while maintaining a consistent design system across 15+ screens."

Outcome

A visually immersive experience with simplified management that received positive feedback for its navigation flow.
